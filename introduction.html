The Person Behind the Diaries
JhoneVic · 2013

Who is JhoneVic?

JhoneVic M. Casaña is a diarist, observer, and chronicler of his own life—a quiet witness to the ordinary and extraordinary moments that shape a person from adolescence into adulthood. Born and raised in the Philippines, he began writing in 2013 at the age of 14, never imagining that his personal notebook entries would one day become a documented record of nearly a decade and a half of growth, heartbreak, resilience, and self-discovery.

The Diaries

JhoneVic's diaries are not polished memoirs or literary exercises. They are raw, unedited, and deeply honest accounts of daily life—written in a natural mix of Filipino and English, often switching between languages mid-sentence, filled with slang, self-deprecating humor, and the unfiltered voice of a young person navigating the chaos of growing up.

2013–2014 (Ages 14–16):

The early years. These diaries capture the intense, world-ending emotions of first love, first heartbreak, and the turbulence of teenage life. At the center is his complicated feelings for a classmate—a relationship that would define much of his emotional landscape during these years. He writes about school, classmates, family, and a local computer shop where he would often go to catch glimpses of her. The tone is dramatic, playful, and deeply earnest— a young person learning what it means to love, lose, and hope.

2026 (Age 27–28):

The adult years. The diary returns after a long silence, and the voice has matured. JhoneVic is now navigating the complexities of adulthood: re-entering a family-owned business, managing mental health struggles with medication, rebuilding strained family relationships, and discovering new romantic feelings for a co-worker. The writing is more contemplative, self-aware, and analytical— a man using the diary as a tool to process anxiety, social awkwardness, and the quiet loneliness of adulthood.

Key Themes

First Love and Heartbreak:

JhoneVic's early diaries are a masterclass in the intensity of first love. The longing, the jealousy, the hope, the rejection—all documented with such vividness that readers can almost feel the emotional whiplash of being a teenager in love for the first time. Even years later, the echoes of that experience shape how he approaches relationships.

Family and Belonging:

Throughout all three years, family is both a source of comfort and frustration. Relationships with cousins evolve from trusted confidantes to complicated boss-figures and occasional rivals. Extended stays with relatives become formative periods of learning and quiet reflection. The diary is, in many ways, a search for belonging—within his family, his workplace, and his own heart.

Mental Health and Self-Awareness:

The 2026 diary marks a significant shift as JhoneVic openly discusses his anxiety and medication. He questions the effects of his treatment, struggles with lethargy, and uses the diary to untangle his own emotions. This is not a story of triumph over mental illness, but a candid, honest record of someone learning to live with it— day by day, entry by entry.

The Act of Writing Itself:

Perhaps the most profound theme is JhoneVic's relationship with his own diary. At 14, it was an emotional outlet—a place to pour out his heart. By 27, it has become a tool for self-analysis, a mirror to reflect on his own growth, and a companion in his solitude. He questions at one point whether he is writing because he is obligated or because he still enjoys it—a moment of meta-awareness that reveals how deeply the diary is woven into his identity.

Final Words

JhoneVic is not a famous author, a public figure, or a polished storyteller. He is simply someone who chose to write down his life—the good days and the bad, the moments of joy and the nights of loneliness, the crushes that faded and the connections that endured.

His diaries are a testament to the power of writing: not as a performance, but as a way to understand oneself. They are proof that the stories of ordinary people, told with honesty and vulnerability, can be just as compelling as any epic. In an age of curated online personas, JhoneVic's diaries are refreshingly, beautifully, unapologetically real.

He is a keeper of fragments—a collector of moments—and through his words, we are invited to witness the quiet, messy, and deeply human process of becoming who we are.

JhoneVic continues to write. His diaries are preserved and transcribed as a personal archive—a legacy of honesty, growth, and the enduring belief that our stories, no matter how small, deserve to be told.
