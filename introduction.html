Who is JhoneVic?

JhoneVic M. Casaña is a diarist, observer, and chronicler of his own life—a quiet witness to the ordinary and extraordinary moments that shape a person from adolescence into adulthood. Born and raised in the Philippines, he began writing in 2013 at the age of 14, never imagining that his personal notebook entries would one day become a documented record of nearly a decade and a half of growth, heartbreak, resilience, and self-discovery.
